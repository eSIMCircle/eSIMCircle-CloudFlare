Wi-Fi and data

Airport Wi-Fi and mobile data tips for the United States, arriving in Miami

Miami traffic can change from smooth to stuck in half an hour, so the best route from the airport depends on live information. That makes working mobile data the most useful thing you can have as you walk out of arrivals.

Published August 17, 2026 · Updated September 1, 2026 · 3 min read · By eSIMCircle Editorial

01

Rail or road from Miami International

OptionRough time to downtownRough cost before publication
Metrorail from the airportAbout 20 to 35 minutes, depending on the stopAround US$2 to US$3 standard fare
Taxi or rideshareAbout 20 to 40 minutesOften around US$20 to US$40 or more to central neighborhoods

Check live road times before choosing. When traffic is heavy, rail can be the faster option; when it is light, a car saves a change of transport.

Heads-upThese figures are planning estimates. Confirm current fares with the operator before you travel.
02

What the airport Wi-Fi is for

Miami International (MIA) offers Wi-Fi in the terminal. Use it to:

  • Compare the rideshare price with the train.
  • Find the rideshare pickup zone, which is often signposted separately from taxis.
  • Message your hotel.

Do not plan on it once you are outside. Rideshare pickups depend on your phone sharing your location with the driver, and that needs mobile data.

03

Turn on your eSIM before you head out

  1. Select the United States eSIM for mobile data.
  2. Turn on data roaming for it.
  3. Leave roaming off on your home SIM.
  4. Turn off any automatic data switching.
  5. Test with a map.

If you are using a dual-SIM phone, your home number stays active for texts, so ride and banking codes still reach you.

04

If you are driving

If you are renting a car, set up navigation before you leave the rental lot. Download an offline map of the Miami area as a backup, and keep the phone mounted rather than in your hand.

05

Small things for the first hour

  • Keep your hotel address and a map pin saved offline.
  • Charge your phone before landing; rideshare apps drain the battery.
  • Check whether your hotel has a shuttle before booking a car.

Quick answers

Is there a train from Miami airport to downtown?
Yes. Metrorail connects the airport with downtown in about 20 to 35 minutes, depending on the stop.
Do I need mobile data for a rideshare pickup at MIA?
Yes, in practice. The app needs your location and lets the driver contact you outside the terminal.
Will my home number work in the US with an eSIM for data?
On a dual-SIM phone, yes. Keep the home SIM for calls and texts.
