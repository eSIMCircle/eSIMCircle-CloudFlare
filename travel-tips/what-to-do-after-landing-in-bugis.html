What to do after landing in Singapore for a stay in Bugis

Bugis is central, lively and well connected, but the MRT from Changi needs at least one change to get there. Here is the order of things that makes the first evening easy.

Published February 2, 2026 · Updated August 18, 2026 · 3 min read · By eSIMCircle Editorial

01

1. Switch your phone over

At Singapore Changi (SIN), use the walk to immigration:

  • Select your Singapore eSIM for mobile data, with roaming on for it.
  • Keep roaming off on your home SIM.
  • Check a map loads. Changi Wi-Fi is there as a fallback.
02

2. Choose MRT or car

OptionRough time to BugisRough cost before publication
MRT, with at least one changeAbout 35 to 50 minutesRoughly S$2 to S$3
Taxi or ride-hailingAbout 20 to 30 minutes in light trafficOften S$20 to S$35 before surcharges
Heads-upPlanning estimates only. Check the current fare and last train time.

The MRT is simple and cheap if you travel light. Contactless bank cards and phone wallets are generally accepted at the gates. With heavy bags or a late arrival, a car is easier.

03

3. Save the right exit

Bugis MRT station has several exits, and the area around it is busy with malls and street markets. Save your hotel's map pin and the nearest exit before you leave Changi.

04

4. First evening in Bugis

Bugis Street market and the surrounding streets are close to the station, and Kampong Glam, with Sultan Mosque and Haji Lane, is a short walk away. If you want a quiet first meal, a working map helps you find somewhere slightly off the main streets.

05

5. Plan tomorrow on hotel Wi-Fi

Download an offline map of central Singapore and check MRT routes for your first full day. Let app updates and photo backups run on the hotel connection.

Quick answers

Is there a direct MRT from Changi to Bugis?
The trip usually needs at least one change. Allow about 35 to 50 minutes.
Can I pay the MRT with my bank card?
Contactless bank cards and phone wallets are generally accepted. Check current rules before you go.
Is Kampong Glam near Bugis?
Yes, it is a short walk from Bugis MRT station.
