How to get from the airport to Perth

Perth's airport is close to the city, and the rail link makes the trip short. The detail that catches people out is the terminal: Perth's terminals are spread out, and your flight's terminal changes how you start.

Published December 5, 2025 · Updated September 1, 2026 · 3 min read · By eSIMCircle Editorial

01

Which terminal?

Perth Airport (PER) has several terminals that are not all next to each other. International flights and some domestic airlines use different ones. Check your booking for the terminal before you land; a terminal transfer can add time before you reach the station or a pickup point.

02

Options into the city

These are planning figures. Fares change, so check Transperth and current taxi rates before you travel.

03

Taking the train

The train is usually the cheapest way into the city and avoids road traffic at busy times. Check where the nearest station is to your terminal and how you will pay. Have your accommodation's nearest station saved, since many hotels are a short walk or a bus ride from it.

04

When a car makes more sense

A taxi or rideshare is often the simpler choice if you are three or four people, have bulky luggage, or are staying outside the CBD, for example in Fremantle or near the beaches. Split between a group, the cost difference shrinks.

Heads-upLate arrivals: check the last train time before you fly. Rideshare pickups use designated zones; follow the signs.
05

Get your phone online

After a long flight into Western Australia, the first thing you need is a working phone. If your Australia eSIM is installed, switch mobile data to it and turn on roaming for that line before you leave the terminal. Keep roaming off on your home SIM.

Check that your map loads and send an arrival message to your accommodation if you are arriving late.

06

First-hour checklist

  • Terminal confirmed and route to the station or pickup zone known.
  • Accommodation address saved offline.
  • eSIM on, map tested.
  • Payment method ready for the train or the car.

Quick answers

Is there a train from Perth Airport to the city?
Yes. The rail link takes about 18 to 25 minutes to the CBD. Check your terminal's nearest station.
Why does the terminal matter at Perth Airport?
The terminals are spread out, so a transfer between them can add time before you reach the train or pickup point.
Is a taxi or rideshare better for groups?
Often, yes. Split between three or four people, the cost difference with the train shrinks.
