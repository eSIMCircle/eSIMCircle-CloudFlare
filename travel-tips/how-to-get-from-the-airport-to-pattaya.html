How to get from the airport to Pattaya

Pattaya has no big international airport of its own. Most visitors land in Bangkok and face a road trip of around two hours, so the first decision is which airport your ticket actually uses.

Published February 23, 2026 · Updated September 15, 2026 · 3 min read · By eSIMCircle Editorial

01

Check the airport code first

Three airports can serve a Pattaya trip, and the transfer changes a lot between them.

AirportCodeWhat it means for Pattaya
Suvarnabhumi, BangkokBKKMost international flights; buses and cars run to Pattaya
Don Mueang, BangkokDMKMany regional and low-cost flights; on the other side of Bangkok, so add time
U-TapaoUTPCloser to Pattaya, but far fewer routes

If your booking says "Bangkok", check which of the first two it is before you plan anything else.

02

From Suvarnabhumi: bus or car

OptionRough timeRough cost before publicationGood for
Airport bus to PattayaAbout 2 hours, traffic permittingAround THB150 to 200Solo travelers, budget trips
Private car or taxiAbout 1.5 to 2 hoursAround THB1,200 to 1,800Groups, late arrivals, door-to-door

Prices and timetables change. Check current fares with the operator, and if you take a taxi, use the official airport taxi service or agree the total fare before you set off.

03

Late-night arrivals

Buses do not run all night. If you land late, check the last departure before you fly. A pre-booked car with clear waiting rules is often less stressful than negotiating at midnight.

Heads-upAsk your hotel in Pattaya whether it offers airport pickups. Some do, and it removes the negotiation entirely.
04

Data for a two-hour transfer

Two hours on the road is a long time without data, especially if your driver needs directions or your hotel needs an arrival message. Switch your Thailand eSIM on before leaving the terminal: choose it for mobile data and turn on roaming for it. Keep roaming off on your home SIM.

Ride-hailing apps such as Grab and Bolt are widely used in Bangkok. If you plan to book through one, sign in at home before the trip.

05

Before you leave the terminal

  • Confirm which airport you are in, and which exit your transport leaves from.
  • Save your hotel address in English and as a map pin.
  • Withdraw or keep a small amount of baht for tolls or tips if your transfer requires it.
  • Test your data with a map.

Quick answers

How far is Pattaya from Bangkok's main airport?
Usually around two hours by road from Suvarnabhumi, depending on traffic.
Is U-Tapao closer to Pattaya?
Yes, but it has far fewer flights. Most international travelers still arrive in Bangkok.
Can I book Grab from Suvarnabhumi to Pattaya?
Ride-hailing is widely used in Bangkok. Check availability and price in the app, and sign in at home before the trip.
