First hour in the United Arab Emirates: Dubai International to Deira

Deira is one of the closest districts to Dubai International Airport, so the transfer itself is short. Most first-hour hassle comes from finding the right metro station or the right branch of a hotel.

Published September 18, 2026 · Updated October 14, 2026 · 3 min read · By eSIMCircle Editorial

01

On the way to passport control

Dubai International (DXB) is large, and the walk from the gate can be long. Use it:

  1. Turn off airplane mode.
  2. Select your UAE eSIM for mobile data and turn on roaming for it.
  3. Keep your home SIM on for SMS, with its data roaming off, so bank and app codes still arrive.
  4. Open a map to confirm data works.
02

Into Deira: metro or taxi

OptionRough timeRough cost before publication
MetroAbout 15 to 30 minutesAround AED3 to 8, depending on zones and card type
TaxiAbout 15 to 25 minutesAround AED35 to 60

The metro is cheap and fast if your hotel is near a station. Deira has several, so the best one depends on your hotel's exact location. Taxis wait in an organised rank outside arrivals.

Heads-upFares change. Check the current metro fare and card options before you travel.
03

Find the right hotel

Deira has many hotels, and some brands have more than one property nearby. Before you fly, save:

  • The full hotel name and street.
  • A map pin.
  • The nearest metro station, if you plan to use the metro.

Show the pin to a taxi driver rather than relying on the neighbourhood name.

04

The first walk

Deira's souks and the creek are close together, and the streets are busy. Walking is often quicker than a car for short trips, especially in the evening. Keep your map handy; street names are not always easy to spot.

05

The first evening

  • Save your hotel Wi-Fi and let large downloads run there.
  • If you plan to visit the souks, keep some cash; many small traders prefer it.
  • Plan any longer trips, such as to Downtown Dubai or the Marina, with the metro map.

Quick answers

How far is Deira from Dubai International Airport?
It is close: around 15 to 25 minutes by taxi or 15 to 30 minutes by metro, depending on your stop.
Which metro station should I use for my Deira hotel?
It depends on the hotel. Check the nearest station on a map before you fly.
Will my home number still receive codes in Dubai?
Yes, if your home SIM stays on for SMS and your carrier supports roaming in the UAE.
