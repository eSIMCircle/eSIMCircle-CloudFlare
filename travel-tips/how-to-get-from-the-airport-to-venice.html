Airport transfer

How to get from the airport to Venice

Venice is the one city where the airport transfer stops before your hotel. Road vehicles end at Piazzale Roma, so the real question is how you cover the last stretch: on foot, by water bus or by water taxi.

Published June 11, 2025 · Updated June 11, 2025 · 3 min read · By eSIMCircle Editorial

01

Know where the road ends

Most international flights land at Venice Marco Polo Airport (VCE), on the mainland. Buses and taxis can take you as far as Piazzale Roma, the car and bus terminus at the edge of the historic centre. From there, everything moves on foot or by boat.

Before you land, find your hotel on a map and ask one question: is it a short walk from Piazzale Roma, or closer to a water bus stop? That decides which transfer makes sense.

02

Compare the three options

OptionRough journey timeRough costBest for
Airport bus to Piazzale RomaAbout 20 minutesAround €10Hotels near Piazzale Roma or the station, light luggage
Water bus from the airportOften 60 to 90 minutesMore than the busHotels near a water bus stop, travelers who want the arrival by water
Private water taxiVaries by stopCan exceed €100Groups, heavy luggage, hotels with their own water entrance

These are planning figures from before publication. Fares and timetables change, so check the operators' current information before you travel.

03

Plan the last few hundred metres

Venice addresses are hard to read and streets twist. A pin dropped on your hotel in a map app is worth more than the written address. Save it offline, along with a screenshot of the route from your arrival point.

Expect bridges with steps. If you are carrying a large suitcase, the walk from Piazzale Roma can take longer than the map suggests.

Heads-upDownload an offline map of Venice before you fly. Narrow streets can confuse GPS, and you will want the map even when the signal is weak.
04

Keep your phone working from the terminal

Airport Wi-Fi is fine for a first look at messages, but it ends at the terminal door. If your eSIM is already installed, switch mobile data to it and turn on roaming for that line before you head to the bus or boat. Then you can check water bus times and follow your map through the streets.

05

Avoid these first-hour mistakes

  • Booking a land taxi and expecting it to reach your hotel. It stops at Piazzale Roma.
  • Buying a water bus ticket without checking whether your stop is on that route.
  • Relying on the written address alone in a city with repeated street names.

Quick answers

Can a taxi take me to my hotel in Venice?
Only if you take a water taxi. Land taxis stop at Piazzale Roma, the edge of the historic centre.
What is the cheapest way from Marco Polo Airport to Venice?
The airport bus to Piazzale Roma is usually the cheapest. From there you walk or take a water bus.
How long is the water bus from the airport?
Often 60 to 90 minutes, depending on your stop. Check the current timetable before you travel.
