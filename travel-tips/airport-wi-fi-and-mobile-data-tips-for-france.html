Airport Wi-Fi and mobile data tips for France, arriving in Lyon

Lyon-Saint Exupéry is well outside the city, and the onward trip involves a tram ride and then another connection. That is three places where you will want your phone, and only the first has airport Wi-Fi.

Published September 21, 2026 · Updated October 9, 2026 · 3 min read · By eSIMCircle Editorial

01

The journey has three legs

LegWhere you areConnection
1Inside the terminalAirport Wi-Fi available
2On the airport tram to Part-DieuMobile data only
3From Part-Dieu to your hotel by metro, tram or on footMobile data only

Lyon-Saint Exupéry (LYS) is linked to the city by the Rhônexpress tram, which runs to Part-Dieu, the main railway station. Before publication the trip took about 30 minutes and a single adult ticket was typically around €16 to €20. A taxi took 30 to 45 minutes and often cost €50 to €70, depending on destination and traffic.

02

What to do on the airport Wi-Fi

Use it while you have it, but do not plan around it:

  • Send your arrival message.
  • Check the next tram departure.
  • Confirm your hotel's nearest metro or tram stop from Part-Dieu.
03

Then switch to mobile data

Before you walk to the tram platform, switch your France eSIM on: select it for mobile data and turn on roaming for that line only. Open a map to confirm it works while you are still inside.

Heads-upPart-Dieu is large and busy. Save your onward route offline so a slow connection in the station does not leave you guessing.
04

If you are carrying on by train

Many visitors to Lyon go on to other parts of France by high-speed train. If that is your plan, screenshot your rail tickets and platform information. Stations announce platforms on screens, but a working phone lets you check delays and seat details without hunting for a board.

05

Data habits for the rest of the trip

  • Let app updates and photo backups run on hotel Wi-Fi.
  • Download offline maps of Lyon's old town, where narrow streets can confuse GPS.
  • If you run low, top up or extend your plan from the phone rather than buying a new one.

Quick answers

How do I get from Lyon airport to the city?
The Rhônexpress tram runs to Part-Dieu station in about 30 minutes. Taxis are also available.
Is there Wi-Fi on the Rhônexpress?
Do not rely on it. Switch to mobile data before boarding so maps work on the way.
Should I install my eSIM before flying to Lyon?
Yes. Install it at home on Wi-Fi and switch it on after landing.
