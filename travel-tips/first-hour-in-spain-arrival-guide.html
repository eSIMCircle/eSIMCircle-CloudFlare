First hour in Spain: an arrival guide for Valencia

Valencia's airport is linked to the city by metro, which makes arrival straightforward in daylight. Late flights are a different story, so this guide splits the first hour into daytime and late-night versions.

Published September 21, 2026 · Updated October 9, 2026 · 3 min read · By eSIMCircle Editorial

01

Before you leave the plane

Switch off airplane mode as you taxi to the gate. If your Spain eSIM is installed, choose it for mobile data and turn on roaming for that line. Keep roaming off on your home SIM. By the time you reach baggage claim, the phone should have found a network.

02

The daytime arrival

StepWhat to doRough time
1Collect bags at Valencia Airport (VLC)15 to 30 minutes
2Walk to the metro station at the airportA few minutes
3Ride the metro into central ValenciaAbout 20 to 30 minutes
4Walk or change to your hotelDepends on location

Before publication, the metro fare including the airport-zone supplement was usually around €5. Check the current fare and which line serves your stop.

03

The late-night arrival

Metro services stop at night. If your flight lands late:

  • Check the last metro time before you fly, not after you land.
  • If you will miss it, plan for a taxi. Before publication, a taxi to the centre took about 15 to 25 minutes and often cost €20 to €30.
  • Message your hotel with your arrival time, especially if reception closes.
Heads-upFigures above are planning estimates from before publication. Fares and timetables change.
04

The first walk

Central Valencia is easy on foot, but the old town's streets wind. Save your hotel as a map pin, not just a street name. If you are staying near the beach or the City of Arts and Sciences, check the tram or bus link from your metro stop.

05

The first evening

Dinner in Spain often starts late. If you arrive in the afternoon, there is time to rest before going out, and a working phone helps you find a place nearby when you do.

06

Common first-hour mistakes

  • Buying a standard metro ticket that does not include the airport supplement.
  • Assuming the metro runs all night.
  • Relying on airport Wi-Fi for directions you will need on the street.

Quick answers

Is there a metro from Valencia airport to the city?
Yes. The metro links the airport with central Valencia in about 20 to 30 minutes.
What if I land in Valencia after the metro stops?
Take a taxi. Check the last metro time before your flight so you know in advance.
Does the airport metro ticket cost more?
Yes, the fare includes an airport-zone supplement. Check the current price at the machine.
