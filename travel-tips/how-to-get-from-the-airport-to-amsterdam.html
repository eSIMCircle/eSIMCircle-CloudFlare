Airport transfer

How to get from the airport to Amsterdam

Schiphol has its own railway station under the terminal, and the train to Amsterdam Centraal takes about a quarter of an hour. For most visitors the transfer is that simple, provided the ticket and the final leg are sorted.

Published June 19, 2026 · Updated September 13, 2026 · 3 min read · By eSIMCircle Editorial

01

The default choice: the train

Amsterdam Airport Schiphol (AMS) has a station directly below the arrivals area. Trains to Amsterdam Centraal run frequently and take around 15 to 20 minutes. Before publication, a one-way fare was roughly €5 to €7.

You can usually tap in at the gates with a contactless bank card or phone wallet, then tap out at your destination. If you use this method, use the same card or phone at both ends, or the system cannot calculate your fare.

Taxis to central Amsterdam were often around €45 to €60 before publication and take 25 to 40 minutes, depending on traffic. Use the official taxi rank outside arrivals.

03

The last leg from Centraal

Centraal is a hub, not a destination. Many hotels are a tram ride or a walk away. Look up your hotel's nearest tram stop before you fly and save the route offline. Trams in Amsterdam also accept contactless tapping, but check the current rules for your card.

Heads-upCyclists have priority on the red bike lanes. When you step out of Centraal, watch where you walk while you look at your map.
04

Phone first, then the train

Switch your phone to mobile data before you go down to the platform. If your eSIM is installed, choose it for data and turn on roaming for that line. Platform changes are announced on screens, but a working connection lets you check the route and the next tram.

05

A short checklist

  • Hotel address and nearest tram stop saved offline.
  • Contactless card or phone wallet ready, or a ticket bought in advance.
  • eSIM switched on before leaving the terminal.

Quick answers

How long is the train from Schiphol to Amsterdam Centraal?
About 15 to 20 minutes. Trains run frequently from the station under the terminal.
Can I pay for the train with a contactless card?
Usually yes. Tap in and out with the same card or phone wallet. Check current rules before you travel.
Is a taxi worth it from Schiphol?
For groups with luggage or hotels far from public transport, often yes. For solo travelers the train is usually faster and cheaper.
