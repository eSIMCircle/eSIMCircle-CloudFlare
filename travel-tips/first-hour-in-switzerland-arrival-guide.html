First hour in Switzerland: from Zurich Airport to Lucerne

Lucerne has no big airport. Most international visitors land at Zurich and take a train for just over an hour. That makes the first hour in Switzerland mostly about two things: the right ticket and a phone that is not quietly roaming on your home plan.

Published September 24, 2025 · Updated November 7, 2025 · 3 min read · By eSIMCircle Editorial

01

First: watch your home SIM

Switzerland is not covered by the EU's roam-like-at-home rules. If your home plan is from an EU country, roaming in Switzerland can be charged extra. As the plane lands at Zurich Airport (ZRH):

  1. Keep data roaming off on your home SIM.
  2. Select your Switzerland eSIM for mobile data.
  3. Turn on roaming for the Switzerland eSIM only.

Check that a map loads before you go to the station.

02

Then: the train to Lucerne

Zurich Airport has its own railway station under the terminal, and Switzerland's rail network makes it the practical gateway for Lucerne.

OptionRough timeRough cost before publication
Train, with any change shown in the timetableAbout 1 hour 5 minutes to 1 hour 20 minutesAround CHF30 to 40 without a travel pass
Car or taxiAbout 1 hourSubstantially more than the train

If you have a Swiss Travel Pass or another rail pass, check whether it already covers the airport-to-Lucerne journey before you buy a ticket.

Heads-upFares and timetables change. Use the official rail timetable for current times and prices.
03

On the train

An hour is enough to sort the evening:

  • Save your hotel as a map pin and check the walk from Lucerne station.
  • Message your hotel with an arrival time.
  • Download an offline map of Lucerne and any mountain trip you are planning.
04

Arriving in Lucerne

The station sits by the lake, close to the old town and the Chapel Bridge, and many hotels are walkable from it. If your hotel is up a hill or across the lake, check the bus or boat connection.

05

What to remember for later

Mountain excursions from Lucerne, such as trips up Pilatus or Rigi, take you into areas where signal can drop. Save tickets and maps before you go up, and carry a power bank in cold weather.

Quick answers

Which airport is closest to Lucerne?
Most international travelers use Zurich Airport, about an hour or a little more by train.
Does my EU mobile plan work in Switzerland at home rates?
Often not. Switzerland is outside EU roam-like-at-home rules, so check your plan or keep home roaming off.
Does a Swiss Travel Pass cover the airport train?
It may. Check your pass terms before buying a separate ticket.
