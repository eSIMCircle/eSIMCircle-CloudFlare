What to do after landing for a trip to York

There is no "York airport" to search for. Visitors fly into a regional airport and finish the journey by train or road. The first job is knowing which airport you are using and how long the onward leg really takes.

Published April 27, 2026 · Updated July 6, 2026 · 3 min read · By eSIMCircle Editorial

01

1. Confirm your gateway

AirportCodeOnward leg to York
ManchesterMANRail or road
Leeds BradfordLBARoad, or road then rail
NewcastleNCLRail or road

Before publication, the onward journey typically took about 1.5 to 2.5 hours depending on the gateway and connection. Rail fares varied a lot with booking time. A car was usually much more expensive than rail for a solo traveler.

Heads-upThese are planning estimates. Check current timetables and fares, and book rail ahead if you can.
02

2. Get online at the airport

Switch your UK eSIM on for mobile data and turn on roaming for that line. Keep roaming off on your home SIM. With data working, you can check live train times and platforms.

03

3. Plan the connection

  • Check whether your airport has a railway station or needs a bus to reach one.
  • Save your train tickets offline.
  • Allow time for delays on the first leg; a missed connection on an advance ticket can be costly.
04

4. Arriving in York

York station is just outside the city walls, and much of the historic centre is walkable from it. Save your hotel's map pin; the medieval streets around the Shambles twist and turn.

05

5. First evening

Walk part of the city walls if daylight allows, or head for the streets around York Minster. Keep your phone charged; the onward journey will have used battery.

06

6. Day trips

York is a base for the Yorkshire Dales and the North York Moors. Coverage in rural areas can be patchy, so download offline maps before heading out.

Quick answers

Does York have an airport?
Not a major commercial one. Common gateways are Manchester, Leeds Bradford and Newcastle.
How long does it take to get to York from the airport?
Typically about 1.5 to 2.5 hours, depending on the airport and connection.
Is train or car better for getting to York?
Rail is usually cheaper for solo travelers. Book ahead, as fares vary with booking time.
