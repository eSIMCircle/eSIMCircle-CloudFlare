Arrival checklist

Germany airport arrival checklist, using Dresden as the example

Dresden Airport is compact and has a railway station directly beneath the terminal. Arrivals here are about as simple as German arrivals get, which makes it a good model for the checklist you can use at any German airport.

Published September 21, 2026 · Updated October 9, 2026 · 3 min read · By eSIMCircle Editorial

01

Before landing

  • ☐ Hotel address and a map pin saved offline.
  • ☐ Germany eSIM installed at home and labelled.
  • ☐ Data roaming off on your home SIM.
  • ☐ Offline map of Dresden downloaded.
02

At the gate and baggage claim

Turn off airplane mode and switch your Germany eSIM on for mobile data, with roaming on for that line. Check that a map loads. If it does not, restart the phone now, while you still have airport Wi-Fi as a fallback.

03

Choosing transport

OptionRough time to central DresdenRough cost before publication
S-Bahn from the station under the terminalAbout 20 to 25 minutesRoughly €3 to €4 for a local ticket
TaxiAbout 20 minutesOften €30 to €40

For most travelers the S-Bahn is the obvious choice: it leaves from directly beneath the terminal and costs a fraction of a taxi. A taxi makes sense for late arrivals, heavy luggage or a group.

Heads-upFigures are planning estimates. Check current fares before you travel.
04

Buying the ticket

  • ☐ Buy a valid local public-transport ticket before boarding.
  • ☐ Validate it if your ticket requires it. Inspectors check, and an unvalidated ticket counts as none.
  • ☐ Keep the ticket until you leave the station at the other end.
05

On the train

Use the ride to message your hotel and check the walk from your stop. Dresden's old town, with the Frauenkirche and the Zwinger, is compact and easy on foot once you get there.

06

At the hotel

  • ☐ Save the hotel Wi-Fi and move big downloads onto it.
  • ☐ Check your plan's remaining data in your eSIM details, if you want a baseline.
  • ☐ Charge the phone before heading out for the evening.
07

This checklist at other German airports

The pattern is the same at Berlin, Munich, Frankfurt or Hamburg: get online before you leave the terminal, buy the right local ticket before boarding, and save your hotel as a map pin. Only the train line and fare change.

Quick answers

How do I get from Dresden Airport to the city?
The S-Bahn leaves from the station directly beneath the terminal and reaches central Dresden in about 20 to 25 minutes.
Do I need to validate my ticket in Germany?
Some tickets must be validated before travel. Check the instructions on your ticket or at the machine.
When should I turn on my Germany eSIM?
At the gate or in baggage claim, before you leave the terminal.
