Airport Wi-Fi and mobile data tips for Germany, landing in Hamburg

Hamburg is one of the easier German arrivals. The S-Bahn leaves from under the terminal and reaches the main station in under half an hour. The two things that slow people down are buying the right ticket and getting the phone online.

Published September 21, 2026 · Updated October 9, 2026 · 3 min read · By eSIMCircle Editorial

01

The route into the city

From Hamburg Airport (HAM), the S1 line is usually the simplest connection to the centre. Before publication it took about 25 minutes to Hamburg Hauptbahnhof and a ticket cost roughly €4 to €5. A taxi took 25 to 35 minutes and often cost €30 to €40.

02

Use the Wi-Fi for the ticket

Hamburg's public transport runs under the HVV network. Make sure you buy the correct HVV fare before boarding; ticket inspectors check, and "I did not know" is not a valid ticket. The airport Wi-Fi is a good place to look up the fare or buy it in the operator's app.

03

Switch to your eSIM before the platform

Underground stations can have weaker signal than the terminal. Turn your Germany eSIM on while you are still upstairs:

  1. Select the Germany eSIM for mobile data.
  2. Turn on data roaming for that line.
  3. Keep roaming off on your home SIM.
  4. Check that a map loads.
04

If the eSIM does not connect

Do not leave the terminal to troubleshoot. While you still have Wi-Fi, toggle airplane mode, restart the phone, and recheck the data line. Contact support from the terminal if it still fails, and keep the eSIM installed.

Heads-upSome Android phones offer automatic data switching between lines. Turn it off so the phone does not quietly use your home SIM.
05

Beyond Hamburg

Long-distance trains from Hamburg Hauptbahnhof run to Berlin, Copenhagen and much of Germany. On long rail journeys the signal can drop between stations, so save tickets and offline maps before you board.

Quick answers

How do I get from Hamburg Airport to the main station?
The S1 S-Bahn runs from under the terminal to Hamburg Hauptbahnhof in about 25 minutes.
Which ticket do I need for the S1 from the airport?
An HVV fare covering the airport. Buy it before boarding and check the current options.
Will my eSIM work in Hamburg's underground stations?
Usually, but signal can be weaker underground. Switch it on in the terminal first.
