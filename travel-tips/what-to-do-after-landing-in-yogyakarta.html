What to do after landing in Yogyakarta

Many older travel posts describe a short trip from Yogyakarta's airport into town. That was the old airport. The current one, YIA, is much further out, so plan the transfer with that in mind.

Published September 21, 2026 · Updated October 9, 2026 · 3 min read · By eSIMCircle Editorial

01

1. Ignore the old advice

Yogyakarta International Airport (YIA) replaced the older Adisutjipto airport for most flights. YIA is much further from central Yogyakarta. If a guide talks about a ten-minute ride to the centre, it is describing the old airport.

02

2. Get online

Select your Indonesia eSIM for mobile data and turn on roaming for it. Keep roaming off on your home SIM. Open a map to confirm.

03

3. Choose the transfer

OptionRough timeRough cost before publication
Airport trainAbout 35 to 45 minutes, plus terminal transfersOften around IDR20,000 to 50,000, depending on class
Car or ride-hailingAbout 60 to 90 minutes, depending on trafficHigher than the train; depends on pickup method
Heads-upPlanning estimates only. Check current fares and the train timetable before you travel.

The train is usually the more predictable choice. A car takes you door to door, which matters with heavy bags or a hotel far from the station.

04

4. Ride-hailing in Yogyakarta

Grab and Gojek are widely used in Indonesian cities. If you set them up at home, you can book a ride from the train station or the airport pickup point as soon as you arrive.

05

5. First evening

Malioboro Street is the busy centre of many first evenings in Yogyakarta. Save your hotel's map pin and keep a little cash for street food and small shops.

06

6. Plan day trips on Wi-Fi

Borobudur and Prambanan are popular day trips. Book tickets and transport on hotel Wi-Fi, save them offline, and download maps for the routes.

Quick answers

How far is Yogyakarta International Airport from the city?
Much further than the old airport. Allow about 35 to 45 minutes by train, or 60 to 90 minutes by road.
Is there a train from YIA to Yogyakarta?
Yes. The airport train is usually the most predictable option.
Can I use Grab or Gojek in Yogyakarta?
Yes, both are widely used. Set them up at home so verification codes reach your home number.
