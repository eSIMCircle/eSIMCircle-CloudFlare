First hour in Vietnam: an arrival guide for Ho Chi Minh City

In Ho Chi Minh City, distance matters less than traffic. A transfer that takes twenty minutes at night can take an hour at rush hour. The first hour goes best if you get online early and only use official pickups.

Published April 8, 2026 · Updated October 9, 2026 · 3 min read · By eSIMCircle Editorial

01

Before you reach the exit

Tan Son Nhat International Airport (SGN) sits inside the city. Once you have cleared immigration:

  1. Select your Vietnam eSIM for mobile data.
  2. Turn on roaming for that line, and keep it off on your home SIM.
  3. Open a map and check the live traffic to your hotel.
02

Choosing the ride

OptionRough time to central districtsRough cost before publication
Local busAbout 30 to 60 minutes, depending on traffic and stopUsually a low local fare
Official taxi or ride-hailingAbout 20 to 45 minutesOften around VND120,000 to 250,000, depending on traffic and district

Use only official taxi queues or a ride-hailing pickup you have confirmed in the app. Ignore offers from people approaching you inside the terminal.

Heads-upThese are planning figures. Prices and traffic change, so check the app's quote before you confirm.
03

Ride-hailing from the airport

Grab is widely used in Ho Chi Minh City. Book it once you have data, and walk to the signposted pickup area. The app shows the driver's plate number; check it before getting in.

04

Arriving at your hotel

District 1 is where many visitors stay first. Streets are busy with motorbikes, and crossing them takes practice: walk at a steady pace and do not stop suddenly. Save your hotel's address and a map pin offline, as signal inside tall buildings can be weaker.

05

The first evening

  • Save the hotel Wi-Fi and let updates run there.
  • Install or open Zalo if you expect to message local businesses; it is widely used in Vietnam.
  • Keep a little cash in small notes for street food and small shops.
06

Common first-hour mistakes

  • Accepting a ride from someone in the arrivals hall.
  • Planning by distance rather than traffic.
  • Waiting until the hotel to get online.

Quick answers

How long does it take from Tan Son Nhat to District 1?
Around 20 to 45 minutes by car, depending heavily on traffic.
Is Grab available at Ho Chi Minh City airport?
Yes. Book in the app once you have data and use the signposted pickup area.
Should I accept a taxi offer inside the terminal?
No. Use official taxi queues or a ride-hailing pickup confirmed in the app.
