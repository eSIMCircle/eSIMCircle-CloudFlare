United Kingdom airport arrival checklist: Edinburgh Airport to the city

Edinburgh's tram makes the airport transfer predictable. Road times are less so, especially on event days. This checklist keeps your arrival on the predictable side.

Published March 17, 2026 · Updated August 10, 2026 · 3 min read · By eSIMCircle Editorial

01

Before you land

  • ☐ UK eSIM installed at home and labelled.
  • ☐ Home SIM data roaming off.
  • ☐ Hotel address, map pin and phone number saved offline.
  • ☐ Offline map of Edinburgh downloaded.
02

In the terminal

  • ☐ Select the UK eSIM for mobile data and turn on roaming for it.
  • ☐ Open a map to check data works.
  • ☐ Check whether any big events are on in the city; road traffic can be much slower.
03

Getting into the city

OptionRough time to central EdinburghRough cost before publication
TramAbout 30 to 40 minutesRoughly £5 to £8
TaxiAbout 25 to 35 minutesOften £25 to £35

The tram is predictable and runs into the city centre. Taxi times vary with traffic and events, such as the August festivals.

Heads-upThese are planning estimates. Check current fares before you travel.
04

The last stretch

Edinburgh is hilly, with the Old Town and New Town on different levels and steps connecting them. A hotel that looks close on a flat map can involve a climb with luggage. Check the walking route, or take a taxi for the final part if your bags are heavy.

05

At the hotel

  • ☐ Save the hotel Wi-Fi.
  • ☐ Message anyone waiting to hear from you.
  • ☐ Check the weather; Edinburgh's can change quickly.
06

If you are travelling on

Trains from Edinburgh Waverley run to York, London and across Scotland. Save tickets offline and check platforms on the station screens.

Quick answers

How do I get from Edinburgh Airport to the city centre?
The tram takes about 30 to 40 minutes. A taxi takes about 25 to 35 minutes, depending on traffic.
Is the tram or a taxi faster during the festival?
The tram is usually more predictable when roads are busy.
Are Edinburgh hotels easy to walk to with luggage?
The city is hilly with steps between levels. Check the walking route before setting off.
