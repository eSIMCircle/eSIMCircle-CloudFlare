Philippines airport arrival checklist: landing at Bohol-Panglao

Bohol's airport is small and the island's beaches are close by. The checklist is short, but two items matter more here than in a big city: confirming where your hotel actually is, and agreeing on a fare before you leave.

Published August 6, 2026 · Updated August 31, 2026 · 3 min read · By eSIMCircle Editorial

01

Before you fly

  • ☐ Find out whether your hotel is on Panglao Island or in Tagbilaran City. Many beach resorts are on Panglao, near the airport; Tagbilaran is further.
  • ☐ Save the hotel address, phone number and a map pin offline.
  • ☐ Install your Philippines eSIM at home and label it.
  • ☐ Turn off data roaming on your home SIM.
02

On landing at Bohol-Panglao (TAG)

  • ☐ Select the Philippines eSIM for mobile data and turn on roaming for it.
  • ☐ Open a map to confirm the distance to your hotel.
  • ☐ Message the hotel with your arrival time if they arrange pickups.
03

04

The fare rule

If a vehicle is not metered, agree the total fare before you get in, and confirm it covers your luggage and the exact destination. This avoids any discussion at the hotel door.

05

After checking in

  • ☐ Save the hotel Wi-Fi.
  • ☐ Download offline maps for day trips, such as the Chocolate Hills or island-hopping boat trips.
  • ☐ Screenshot any tour or boat bookings.
06

A note on signal

Coverage is usually fine around Panglao's main beach areas and in Tagbilaran. On boat trips and in more remote inland areas, signal can drop. Keep important details offline so a gap does not matter.

Quick answers

Is my Bohol hotel on Panglao or in Tagbilaran?
Check your booking address. Many beach resorts are on Panglao near the airport; Tagbilaran is the main city and further away.
Should I agree the fare before getting in?
Yes, if the vehicle is not metered. Confirm the total and the exact destination first.
Will my eSIM work on island-hopping trips?
It works where there is coverage. At sea and on remote islands signal can drop, so save bookings offline.
