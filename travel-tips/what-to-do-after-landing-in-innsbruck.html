What to do after landing in Innsbruck

Innsbruck has one of the shortest airport transfers in the Alps. Ten or twenty minutes after leaving the terminal you can be in town, which leaves the first evening free for planning your mountain days.

Published October 7, 2025 · Updated April 6, 2026 · 3 min read · By eSIMCircle Editorial

01

1. Get online before you leave the terminal

At Innsbruck Airport (INN), switch your Austria eSIM on for mobile data and turn on roaming for that line. Keep roaming off on your home SIM. Open a map to confirm.

02

2. Bus or taxi

OptionRough time to the centreRough cost before publication
Local busAbout 15 to 20 minutesRoughly €3 for a local ticket
TaxiAbout 10 to 15 minutesOften €15 to €25
Heads-upPlanning estimates only. Check the current fare before you travel.

Because the airport is so close, a taxi can make sense for several travelers or for anyone carrying ski or hiking gear. Alone with a small bag, the bus is the obvious choice.

03

3. Find your hotel

The old town is compact, with the Golden Roof at its centre, and many hotels are within walking distance of the main bus stops. Save your hotel's map pin; some old-town lanes are pedestrian only.

04

4. Plan the mountain days on hotel Wi-Fi

Innsbruck is surrounded by mountains reached by cable cars and funiculars. Before the first trip up:

  • Download offline maps for the areas you will visit.
  • Save lift or cable car tickets as screenshots.
  • Check the weather forecast for the altitude, not just the town.
05

5. Look after your battery

Cold air drains phone batteries faster, and a phone searching for signal on a mountain uses more power. Carry a power bank on mountain days and keep the phone in an inside pocket.

06

6. If you are moving on

Innsbruck sits on rail routes to Salzburg, Vienna, Munich and northern Italy. Save your train tickets offline and check platforms at the station.

Quick answers

How far is Innsbruck Airport from the city centre?
It is close. About 10 to 15 minutes by taxi or 15 to 20 minutes by bus.
Is a taxi worth it from Innsbruck Airport?
For several travelers or heavy gear, often yes, because the distance is short.
Does my eSIM work on the mountains around Innsbruck?
Usually where there is coverage, but signal can drop in high or remote areas. Save maps and tickets offline.
