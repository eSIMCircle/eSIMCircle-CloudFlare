What to do after landing in Beijing

Beijing has two major airports on opposite sides of the city, and China's internet works differently from most countries. The first hour after landing is about getting both of those right.

Published September 21, 2026 · Updated October 9, 2026 · 3 min read · By eSIMCircle Editorial

01

1. Confirm your airport

AirportCodeLocation
Beijing Capital InternationalPEKNorth-east of the city
Beijing Daxing InternationalPKXFar south of the city

They are not interchangeable, and the transfers are different. Save your arrival airport code and your hotel name in Chinese before leaving the terminal.

02

2. Get online

Select your China eSIM for mobile data and turn on roaming for it. Keep roaming off on your home SIM.

Many apps visitors use, such as Google services and WhatsApp, are blocked on local Chinese networks. Travel eSIMs that route data outside China can usually reach them. Test the ones you need now, in the terminal, so you know what works before you rely on it.

03

3. Test the local apps

If you set up WeChat or Alipay before the trip, open them and confirm they work. Many taxis, shops and restaurants in Beijing expect mobile payment.

04

4. Choose transport

OptionRough timeRough cost before publication
Airport express rail, then metroAbout 20 to 40 minutes to the first central interchange, plus metro timeCommonly RMB25 to 35 for the express segment
TaxiAbout 45 to 90 minutes, depending on airport and trafficSignificantly higher than rail and varies by distance
Heads-upThese are planning estimates. Check current fares before you travel.
05

5. Show the address in Chinese

Taxi drivers may not read English. Show your hotel's name and address in Chinese, or a map pin, rather than saying it aloud.

06

6. At the hotel

Save the hotel Wi-Fi. Download offline maps and your translation app's Chinese pack if you have not already.

Quick answers

Which Beijing airport am I flying into?
Check the airport code on your booking: PEK is Capital, PKX is Daxing. They are on opposite sides of the city.
Will Google and WhatsApp work after I land in Beijing?
They are blocked on local networks. Travel eSIMs that route data outside China can usually reach them; test them in the terminal.
Should I have my hotel address in Chinese?
Yes. It helps taxi drivers and local map apps find it.
