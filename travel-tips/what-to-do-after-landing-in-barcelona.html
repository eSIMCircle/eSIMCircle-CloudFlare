What to do after landing in Barcelona

At Barcelona-El Prat, the first thing to check is not your phone or your bag. It is your terminal. Terminal 1 and Terminal 2 have different transport options, and choosing the wrong one adds a shuttle ride to your evening.

Published January 10, 2026 · Updated September 11, 2026 · 3 min read · By eSIMCircle Editorial

01

1. Check your terminal

The airport train serves Terminal 2 directly. If you land at Terminal 1, you need the airport shuttle bus to reach the train, or another way into the city such as the airport bus or a taxi. Your boarding pass or airline app shows the terminal.

02

2. Get your phone online

While you wait for bags:

  • Select your Spain eSIM for mobile data.
  • Turn on roaming for it and keep it off on your home SIM.
  • Open a map to confirm.

04

4. Know your neighbourhood

Barcelona's central districts feel different. The Gothic Quarter and El Born have narrow lanes where taxis cannot always reach the door. The Eixample has a wide grid that is easy to navigate. Save your hotel as a map pin either way.

05

5. Look after your phone

Busy areas such as La Rambla and crowded metro carriages are known for pickpockets. Keep your phone in a front pocket or a zipped bag, especially while you are distracted by a map.

06

6. Plan dinner late

Dinner in Barcelona often starts after 9pm. If you land in the afternoon, there is time to check in, rest, and find a place nearby. A working phone helps when you are looking for somewhere open late.

Quick answers

Does the train serve both terminals at Barcelona airport?
The train serves Terminal 2 directly. From Terminal 1, take the airport shuttle or use another transfer option.
How long does it take to reach central Barcelona?
About 30 to 45 minutes by public transport or 25 to 40 minutes by taxi.
Is my phone at risk of pickpocketing in Barcelona?
Busy tourist areas and crowded transport have a reputation for it. Keep your phone secure while using maps.
