Japan airport arrival checklist: Haneda or Narita to Shinjuku

"Tokyo airport" means two very different trips. From Haneda, Shinjuku is under an hour by rail. From Narita, it can be closer to two. This checklist starts with that difference.

Published October 6, 2026 · Updated October 8, 2026 · 3 min read · By eSIMCircle Editorial

01

Check 1: which airport?

AirportCodeRough rail time to ShinjukuRough rail cost before publication
HanedaHNDAbout 35 to 55 minutesGenerally under ¥1,000
NaritaNRTAbout 75 to 100 minutesExpress options commonly several thousand yen

Taxis from Narita are very expensive compared with rail, and the drive is long. From Haneda a taxi is more reasonable but still depends on traffic.

Heads-upThese are planning estimates. Check the operators' current fares and timetables.
02

Check 2: phone online

Before passport control, or while you wait:

  • ☐ Japan eSIM selected for mobile data.
  • ☐ Roaming on for the Japan eSIM, off for your home SIM.
  • ☐ A map loads.

Japan's mobile coverage is strong, so if data does not start, restart the phone and recheck the data line.

03

Check 3: how you will pay for transport

  • ☐ A transit IC card, either a physical one or one added to your phone wallet if supported.
  • ☐ Or a ticket for an airport express, if you prefer a reserved seat.

IC cards cover most trains and subways in Tokyo and save queuing for single tickets at each journey.

04

Check 4: the Shinjuku station problem

Shinjuku Station is one of the busiest stations in the world, with many exits. Before you arrive:

  • ☐ Find which exit is closest to your hotel.
  • ☐ Save your hotel's map pin offline.
  • ☐ Note the hotel name in Japanese if you can, for a taxi as a backup.
05

Check 5: luggage

Large suitcases on crowded trains at rush hour are hard work. Japan has luggage forwarding services that send bags from the airport to your hotel; consider one if you are arriving at a busy time.

06

At the hotel

  • ☐ Save the hotel Wi-Fi.
  • ☐ Download offline maps for the next day's plans.
  • ☐ Charge the phone; navigation and transit apps use battery fast.

Quick answers

Is Haneda or Narita closer to Shinjuku?
Haneda is much closer. Rail from Haneda takes about 35 to 55 minutes; from Narita about 75 to 100 minutes.
Can I use an IC card from the airport?
Yes. IC cards work on most trains and subways in Tokyo, including many airport routes.
How do I find the right exit at Shinjuku Station?
Check your hotel's nearest exit on a map before you arrive and save it offline.
