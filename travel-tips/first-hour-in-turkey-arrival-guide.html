First hour in Turkey: an arrival guide for Antalya

"Antalya" on a hotel booking can mean the city centre or a resort many kilometres along the coast. Your first hour in Turkey goes smoothly once you know which.

Published September 21, 2026 · Updated October 9, 2026 · 3 min read · By eSIMCircle Editorial

01

The question to answer before landing

Is your hotel in central Antalya, or in a resort area such as Lara, Kundu or Belek? The airport (AYT) sits between them, and the right transport is completely different for each.

Where you are stayingTransfer to think about
Central Antalya, including the old townLocal transit or a taxi
Lara or KunduTaxi or a hotel transfer
Belek or further along the coastPre-booked transfer or taxi; check the distance
02

While you wait for bags

Turn off airplane mode. If your Turkey eSIM is installed, select it for mobile data and turn on roaming for that line. Keep roaming off on your home SIM. Then open a map and check the distance to your hotel.

03

Getting into central Antalya

Before publication:

  • Local transit to central areas took about 40 to 55 minutes for a low fare. Check the current AntalyaKart price and how to pay before boarding.
  • A taxi took about 20 to 35 minutes, with the fare depending on the meter, the district and exchange rates.
Heads-upTreat these as planning estimates and check live information before you travel.
04

Getting to the resorts

Many resort hotels offer transfers. If yours does, book it before you fly; it removes any negotiation at the kerb. If you take a taxi, check the meter is running, or agree the price before you set off.

05

Your first evening

Once you have checked in:

  • Save the hotel's Wi-Fi details and let updates run there.
  • If you plan day trips to the old town (Kaleiçi), the waterfalls or ancient sites, download offline maps for each.
  • Keep your hotel's name and address handy in Turkish as well as English for taxi drivers.
06

Common first-hour mistakes

  • Assuming a resort near Belek is a short ride because the booking says Antalya.
  • Joining a taxi without checking the meter.
  • Waiting until the hotel to sort data, then needing it for the transfer.

Quick answers

How far is Antalya airport from the city centre?
Central Antalya is roughly 20 to 35 minutes by taxi, or 40 to 55 minutes by local transit. Resort areas can be much further.
Should I book a transfer to my Antalya resort?
If your hotel offers one, booking ahead is usually the simplest choice, especially for resorts along the coast.
Can I pay local transit with cash?
Check how fares are paid before boarding. Antalya transit uses a card system, so confirm the current options.
