Singapore airport arrival checklist: Changi to Chinatown

Changi is famously easy, and Chinatown is well served by the MRT. The checklist is mostly about timing: the MRT is cheap and simple until the last train leaves.

Published September 21, 2026 · Updated October 9, 2026 · 3 min read · By eSIMCircle Editorial

01

In the terminal

  • ☐ Singapore eSIM selected for mobile data, roaming on for that line.
  • ☐ Home SIM data roaming off.
  • ☐ A map loads.

Changi's free Wi-Fi is a good fallback while you do this.

02

Transport to Chinatown

OptionRough timeRough cost before publication
MRTAbout 40 to 55 minutesRoughly S$2 to S$3
Taxi or ride-hailingAbout 20 to 30 minutes in light trafficOften S$20 to S$35 before surcharges

The MRT usually involves a change of line on the way to Chinatown. Contactless bank cards and phone wallets are generally accepted at the gates, so you may not need to buy a separate card.

Heads-upPlanning figures only. Check current fares and the last train time before you fly.
03

If you land late

  • ☐ Check the last MRT time before you fly.
  • ☐ If you will miss it, compare the official taxi queue with ride-hailing pickup points.
  • ☐ Message your hotel if reception hours are limited.
04

The last few minutes

Chinatown's streets are busy and full of small shops and food stalls. Save your hotel as a map pin and note the nearest MRT exit. Signal is generally good, but tall buildings can make GPS jump, so the exit name helps.

05

At the hotel

  • ☐ Save the hotel Wi-Fi.
  • ☐ Download an offline map of central Singapore.
  • ☐ Plan the next morning's MRT routes while you are on Wi-Fi.

Quick answers

How do I get from Changi to Chinatown?
The MRT takes about 40 to 55 minutes, usually with one change. A taxi or ride-hailing takes about 20 to 30 minutes in light traffic.
Can I pay for the MRT with a contactless card?
Contactless bank cards and phone wallets are generally accepted. Check current rules before you travel.
What if I arrive after the last MRT?
Use the official taxi queue or a ride-hailing pickup point.
