Airport Wi-Fi and mobile data tips for South Korea, heading to Gangnam

Incheon is one of the easiest airports in the world to navigate, and its Wi-Fi is good. The tricky part comes later: Gangnam is south of the river, a long way from the airport, and the best route depends on exactly where your hotel is.

Published September 18, 2025 · Updated September 18, 2025 · 3 min read · By eSIMCircle Editorial

01

Which airport?

Most international flights to Seoul use Incheon International Airport (ICN). Some regional flights use Gimpo (GMP), which is much closer to the city. Check your booking before planning the transfer.

02

Bus or rail to Gangnam?

OptionRough time from IncheonRough cost before publicationBest when
Airport limousine busAbout 60 to 90 minutesWithin ₩5,000 to 20,000 range for bus or rail optionsYour hotel is near one of the bus stops
Airport rail plus subwayAbout 60 to 90 minutes with connectionsWithin the same rangeYou travel light and do not mind changes
TaxiAbout 60 to 90 minutesOften ₩70,000 to 100,000 or moreLate arrival, groups, heavy luggage

Gangnam sits south of the Han River, so airport limousine buses can be the more convenient choice for hotels near their stops, even when rail looks faster on paper.

03

Do this on the airport Wi-Fi

  • Find the limousine bus number and stop closest to your hotel, or your rail route.
  • Save your hotel address in Korean as well as English. Taxi drivers and map apps both find it more easily.
  • Download or update a local map app such as Naver Map or KakaoMap. Google Maps has limited directions in Korea.
04

Then switch to mobile data

Select your South Korea eSIM for mobile data, turn on roaming for that line, and keep roaming off on your home SIM. Open your map app to confirm it finds your location.

Heads-upKorean cities have dense coverage, including much of the subway. If data fails at the airport, it is almost always a setting, so recheck the data line before anything else.
05

On the way in

A 60 to 90 minute journey is long enough to message your hotel with an arrival time and check the walk from your stop. If you are taking a taxi, the Kakao T app is widely used; set it up at home, since sign-up may need a verification code.

Quick answers

Is the airport bus or train better for Gangnam?
It depends on your hotel. Limousine buses stop near many Gangnam hotels, so they can be more convenient even if rail looks faster.
Does Google Maps work for directions in Seoul?
It has limited directions in Korea. Many visitors use Naver Map or KakaoMap instead.
Should I save my hotel address in Korean?
Yes. It helps taxi drivers and local map apps find it quickly.
