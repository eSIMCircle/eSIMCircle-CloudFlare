How to get from the airport to Bordeaux

Bordeaux-Mérignac sits west of the city, and the tram line that serves it makes the trip simple and cheap. A taxi saves time only when the tram does not suit your hotel or your luggage.

Published July 8, 2026 · Updated October 2, 2026 · 3 min read · By eSIMCircle Editorial

01

Your options at a glance

Fares and services change, so confirm current details with the local transport operator before your trip.

02

Taking the tram

The tram is the default choice for central stays. It is slower than a taxi in light traffic but costs a fraction of the price, and it delivers you into the centre without the risk of a traffic jam on the ring road.

Two things to sort before boarding:

  1. Buy the right ticket. Standard public transport tickets cover the trip; check the current options at the machine or in the operator's app.
  2. Validate or activate it. An unvalidated ticket counts as no ticket during inspections.
03

When to take a taxi

A taxi makes sense if you arrive late, travel with several people, or carry heavy bags to a hotel far from a tram stop. Look at your hotel's location first: Bordeaux's old centre has pedestrian streets, so a taxi may still drop you a short walk away.

Heads-upSave your hotel as a map pin, not just a street name. Several Bordeaux streets share similar names across districts.
04

Get online before you leave the terminal

Bordeaux-Mérignac is a mid-sized airport, and its Wi-Fi does not follow you onto the tram. Switch your phone to your travel eSIM before walking out: choose it for mobile data and turn on roaming for that line. With data working, you can check the tram's live times and the walk from your stop.

05

First-hour mistakes to avoid

  • Boarding the tram without validating the ticket.
  • Taking a taxi without checking whether the driver can reach a pedestrian street.
  • Relying on airport Wi-Fi for the onward route.

Quick answers

Is there a tram from Bordeaux airport to the city?
Yes. The tram is usually the simplest and cheapest way into central Bordeaux.
How long does the tram take?
Around 35 to 45 minutes to the centre. Check the current timetable before travel.
Do I need to validate my tram ticket?
Yes. Validate or activate it before you travel to avoid a fine.
