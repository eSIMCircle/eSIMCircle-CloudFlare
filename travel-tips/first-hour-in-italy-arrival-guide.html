Your first hour in Italy: an arrival guide, starting in Bologna

Bologna's airport is close to the city, which makes it a good place to see how a calm first hour in Italy works. Here is that hour, broken into the moments where a small decision saves time.

Published May 31, 2026 · Updated October 14, 2026 · 3 min read · By eSIMCircle Editorial

01

Minutes 0 to 20: off the plane and through the airport

At Bologna Guglielmo Marconi Airport (BLQ), most of the first twenty minutes are queues: passport control if you are arriving from outside the Schengen area, then baggage claim. Use the wait.

  • Turn off airplane mode and let the phone find a network.
  • If your Italy eSIM is installed, select it for mobile data and turn on roaming for that line. Keep roaming off on your home SIM.
  • Check that a map loads.
02

Minutes 20 to 30: choose the ride

OptionRough timeRough cost before publicationNote
Marconi ExpressAbout 7 to 10 minutesRoughly €12 to €13 one wayAn airport service, priced above a normal local ride
TaxiAbout 15 to 25 minutesOften €20 to €30 to central areasDoor to door, simpler with bags

The airport is close to the city, so neither option is long. The Marconi Express runs to the main railway station; if your hotel is in the historic centre you will walk or take a short bus from there. For two or more people with luggage going to the centre, a taxi can be similar in total cost.

Heads-upFigures are planning estimates. Check current fares before you travel.
03

Minutes 30 to 45: the last stretch

Bologna's historic centre is compact and much of it is under covered porticoes, which makes walking pleasant even in rain. Some streets have traffic restrictions, so a taxi may drop you near rather than at your door. A saved map pin for the hotel takes care of the last few hundred metres.

04

Minutes 45 to 60: settle in

  • Message anyone waiting to hear you have arrived.
  • Save the hotel's Wi-Fi and let big app updates run there.
  • If you are taking trains onward to Florence, Venice or Milan, check your ticket and platform details now, while you are calm and connected.
05

What tends to go wrong

Forgetting that the Marconi Express is an airport service with its own fare, and being surprised at the machine. Booking a taxi to a restricted street. Using hotel Wi-Fi as the only plan for the first evening, then needing a map at dinner.

Quick answers

How do I get from Bologna airport to the city?
The Marconi Express runs to the main railway station in about 7 to 10 minutes. Taxis take about 15 to 25 minutes.
Is the Marconi Express cheaper than a taxi?
For one person, yes. For a group with luggage, a taxi can cost a similar amount in total.
When should I switch on my Italy eSIM?
As soon as you land, while you wait at passport control or baggage claim.
