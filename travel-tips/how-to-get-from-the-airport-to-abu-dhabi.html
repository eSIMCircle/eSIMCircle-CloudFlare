How to get from the airport to Abu Dhabi

Abu Dhabi is spread out, and the airport sits well outside the main island. Official taxis are easy to find, buses cost less, and either way the most useful thing you can carry is your hotel's exact name and district.

Published September 8, 2026 · Updated September 8, 2026 · 3 min read · By eSIMCircle Editorial

01

The two main options

International flights arrive at Zayed International Airport (AUH).

OptionRough timeRough cost before publicationNotes
Public busAbout 45 to 60 minutesLow compared with taxis; depends on routeCheck how fares are paid before boarding
Official taxiAbout 30 to 40 minutesAround AED70 to 100 to central areasClearly organised at the airport

Treat these figures as a guide and check current fares before you travel.

02

Know your hotel's exact name

Many hotel brands have more than one property in Abu Dhabi, sometimes in very different districts. Before you fly, save:

  • The full hotel name, including the area (for example, the island or district).
  • The address in English.
  • A map pin.

Show the pin to the taxi driver rather than just saying the brand name.

03

Taking a taxi

Official taxis wait in an organised rank outside arrivals. They are metered. If you are travelling with a group or arriving late, a taxi is usually the simplest choice.

04

Taking the bus

Buses are cheaper but slower, and you may need to change for some districts. Check the route to your hotel's nearest stop in advance, and find out how fares are paid; some systems use a card rather than cash.

Heads-upIf you are travelling between Abu Dhabi and Dubai on the same trip, plan that leg separately. It is a long road journey, not a city transfer.
05

Phone online before the taxi rank

Switch your UAE eSIM on before you walk out: select it for mobile data and turn on roaming for that line. Keep your home SIM on for SMS so bank and app codes still reach you. With data working, you can share your live location with your hotel or check the route against the driver's.

06

Quick checks

  • Hotel name, district and map pin saved.
  • eSIM switched on and a map tested.
  • Payment method ready.

Quick answers

How long is a taxi from Abu Dhabi airport to the city?
Around 30 to 40 minutes to central areas, depending on traffic and district.
Are taxis at Zayed International Airport metered?
Official airport taxis are metered and wait in an organised rank outside arrivals.
Why do I need my hotel's district?
Several hotel brands have multiple properties in Abu Dhabi. The district and a map pin avoid going to the wrong one.
