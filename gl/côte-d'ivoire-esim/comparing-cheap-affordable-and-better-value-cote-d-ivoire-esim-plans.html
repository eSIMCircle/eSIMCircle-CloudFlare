01

Read the trip before you read the price

A low price only helps when the plan fits the trip. For Côte D'Ivoire, start with the moments when mobile data matters most: airport transfer, map use, hotel check-in, ride apps, translation, ticket screens, and messages to people waiting for you. If those moments fall on long travel days, choose validity and data margin before comparing small price differences.

02

Setup timing for Côte D'Ivoire

Install the eSIM profile on steady Wi-Fi before the trip gets busy. Keep the QR email available offline, leave your primary SIM in place if you need calls or bank messages, and select the travel eSIM only for mobile data when you are ready to use it. If data does not register right away, restart the phone and check roaming on the eSIM line before changing advanced settings.

  • Save the QR email somewhere you can open without mobile data.
  • Check eSIM support and carrier unlock status before purchase.
  • Count arrival and departure days in the validity window.
  • Use Wi-Fi for app updates, cloud backups, and long video calls.
03

Choosing between daily flexibility and fixed data

Before checkout, confirm that the phone is unlocked and supports eSIM, that the plan covers Côte D'Ivoire, and that the validity window includes arrival and departure days. Hotspot sharing, video calls, cloud backups, and laptop work all change the right allowance. A smaller plan can be enough for maps and messaging, but it needs to match the real route.
