This collection holds 16 pieces on mobile data for Asia (7 areas). They start with the first hour after landing and run through cost, comparing plans and the choice between daily and fixed allowances, so you can begin at the question that is closest to your own.

The plan list for Asia (7 areas) currently shows 32 plans: 8 priced per day, from 2.99 USD with allowances of 500MB to 10GB per day; 24 with a fixed allowance, from 4.55 to 170.55 USD and validity of 7, 15, 30, 90 or 180 days. Prices were listed when the pieces were last updated, and the plan page always carries the current price, coverage and hotspot terms.

Daily plans are sized per day and suit hotspot sharing, uploads, video calls and heavy navigation. Fixed-data plans are one allowance for the whole validity period and suit predictable trips built around maps, messaging and browsing. Every plan can be topped up or extended while it is active, so the first choice does not have to be perfect.

Before you buy, check that your phone supports eSIM and is carrier unlocked. The compatible phones list and the installation guide cover both.
